Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Only two of these are full towel warmers. The Tukmotu 35L and Parquix 38L are the only standalone heated units here; the other five are replacement fragrance discs that only work inside a bucket-style warmer you already own.
  • Capacity varies by liters, not just towel count. The Tukmotu holds up to 3 bath towels in its 35L drum, while the Parquix's 38L interior is sized for oversized towels, blankets, and robes.
  • Auto shut-off is standard on both warmers. The Tukmotu offers 3 timer settings and the Parquix has 4 temperature modes, so neither one is meant to run indefinitely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a budget heated towel rack cost?

The two genuine towel warmers in this guide, the Tukmotu 35L and the Parquix 38L, cost $39.99 and $45.11. Replacement fragrance discs, which are accessories rather than standalone warmers, run $6.99 to $17.99 depending on pack size.

Are fragrance discs the same as a towel warmer?

No. Fragrance discs are a refill accessory that sits inside a bucket-style towel warmer you already own; they don't generate heat on their own. If you don't have a compatible warmer yet, start with a full unit like the Tukmotu or Parquix before buying discs.

Do budget towel warmers use much electricity?

Foldable bucket-style warmers like the ones here are designed for short cycles rather than continuous use, and both the Tukmotu and Parquix include auto shut-off so they don't run indefinitely, which keeps typical electricity use low.
