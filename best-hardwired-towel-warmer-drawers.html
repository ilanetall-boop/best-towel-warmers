Things to Know Before You Buy

  • "Hardwired" usually means convertible. Many hardwired towel warmer drawers and racks ship as plug-in 120V units you can later wire into the wall. Check the model before you call an electrician.
  • Rack versus drawer. Rack models hang towels flat and dry them between uses. Drawer and bucket styles, like the 5L and 8L units here, heat one towel inside an enclosed cabinet.
  • Stainless steel beats carbon steel. Every pick in this guide uses stainless steel, which shrugs off the humidity and rust that kill cheaper warmers within a season.
  • Capacity scales with size and price. A 9-bar wall rack warms more towels than a 5-bar freestanding unit, but it costs more and needs wall space.
  • Set your budget first. These picks run from $69.99 to $228.88, so decide what you'll spend before the biggest model tempts you.

We looked past these seven picks at a wide field of hardwired towel warmer drawers and racks, and a few categories didn't make the cut. Frequently Asked Questions

Are the best hardwired towel warmer drawers actually hardwired?

Most units sold as hardwired towel warmer drawers and racks ship as plug-in 120V models that you can convert to a permanent wall connection. An electrician removes the plug and wires the unit into a switched circuit. Every pick in this guide plugs in out of the box, so you can run it today and hardwire it later if you want a cleaner, cordless look.

How much does it cost to run a hardwired towel warmer?

A towel warmer draws modest power, closer to a couple of light bulbs than a space heater. If you run it for an hour or two around your shower, the cost stays low, a few dollars a month for most people. The enclosed drawer-style units, like the 5L and 8L models here, cycle on and off, which keeps their draw down further. Adding an outlet timer trims the cost even more.

Drawer-style or rack-style towel warmer: which should I buy?

Choose a drawer or bucket style, like the VERYTOP 5L or NOVAL 8L, if you want one very hot towel inside an enclosed cabinet and you have little wall space. Choose a rack, like our Colliford top pick or the 9-bar DAWEYEAL, if you want to warm and dry several towels at once. Racks dry towels between uses, while drawers heat a single towel fast right before you step out of the shower.
