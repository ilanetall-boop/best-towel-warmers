Things to Know Before You Buy

  • You add the scent yourself. A towel warmer with fragrance diffuses whatever essential oil you place inside it. Drop the oil on a cotton pad, tuck the pad between the towels, and let the heat spread the scent.
  • Enclosed formats hold fragrance, open racks lose it. Lidded buckets and cabinets keep the scented air around the towel, while wall racks and rotating racks let it drift into the room within minutes.
  • Expect to pay $59.99 to $159.99. The wall rack sits at the bottom of that range, the buckets and cabinets cluster between $69.99 and $99.99, and the ENZE smart rack tops it.
  • All seven picks use stainless steel. A humid bathroom corrodes coated frames first, and steel also wipes clean if an oil drop lands on it.
  • Capacity decides the format. A bucket warms a towel or two for one person, and a rack serves a family bathroom where several towels need heat at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do towel warmers come with fragrance built in?

Most do not ship with scent included. A towel warmer with fragrance works as a diffuser: you add a few drops of essential oil to a cotton pad, place it near the towels, and the heat spreads the smell. That means you choose the fragrance and change it whenever you like.

Can I put essential oil directly on my towels?

We recommend the cotton pad method instead. Undiluted oil can stain fabric and leave slick spots, while a pad set beside the folded towel scents it evenly and keeps the oil off the fibers. Two or three drops per cycle is plenty inside a lidded bucket like our SereneLife pick.

Is a bucket or a rack better for scented towels?

A bucket wins for scent. The closed lid traps warm, fragrant air against the towel, while a rack like the ENZE or the LAMATILOVE lets the scent drift into the room within minutes. Choose a rack only when you need several towels warmed at once, and treat the fragrance as secondary; our buying guide walks through the formats in more depth.
