Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take a towel warmer to heat up?
Most of the stainless steel models in this price range reach usable heat within a few minutes and full temperature inside 10 to 15 minutes. Bucket-style units like the Keenray and SereneLife warm faster because the towel sits closer to the heating element than it does in a taller drum design.
Do towel warmers use a lot of electricity?
No. Every pick here plugs into a standard outlet and draws roughly the same power as a hair dryer, but only while it's actively heating. Auto shut-off timers, which most of these models include, cut that draw further by switching off once your towel is warm.
Are bucket towel warmers or drum towel warmers better?
It depends on what you're warming. Bucket-style warmers like the Keenray Bucket Style and PureClean hold bulkier items such as robes and blankets, while taller drum models like the Keenray Towel Warmer Large Towel and Asuka are built around standard bath towel dimensions and take up less floor space.
